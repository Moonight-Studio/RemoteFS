Built around your storage

Remote file traffic goes directly between your device and the server you choose. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not route your files through developer-operated servers.

Trial and purchase

Version 1.2.0 test builds introduce an optional seven-day free trial followed by a one-time lifetime unlock. The trial does not automatically charge you or become a subscription. Availability and pricing depend on the version and storefront. TestFlight purchases are test transactions and do not carry over to the App Store version.
